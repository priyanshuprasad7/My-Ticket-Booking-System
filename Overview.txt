PROJECT IDEA
==================================================

TRAIN STOPPAGES
==================================================

This is an important part of the project.

A train can have multiple stoppages.

Example:

Train 1001:

1. Ara
2. Patna
3. Hajipur
4. Muzaffarpur
5. Darbhanga
6. Madhubani

The order matters.

If the user searches:

Ara → Madhubani

Train 1001 is valid.

But if another train route is:

Madhubani
↓
Darbhanga
↓
Muzaffarpur
↓
Patna
↓
Ara

Then that train should NOT be considered valid for:

Ara → Madhubani

because Ara occurs after Madhubani in that train's route.
